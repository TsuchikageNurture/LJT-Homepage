# Junteng Liu

## Personal Details

- First-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group.
- Research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
- Research interests include LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning.
- Research interests include Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM).
- Research interests include LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability.

## Academic Background

- Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.
- PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group; PhD supervisor: Professor Junxian He.
- HKUST NLP Group is part of Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and is led by Professor Junxian He.
- B.Eng. (2020-2024), Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
- Graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024.
- Previously advised by Professor Junxian He during undergraduate studies at SJTU.

## Research Experience

- PhD candidate, HKUST NLP Group, 2024-Present.
- Research Intern at MINIMAX (February 2025 - Present).
- Research Intern at Tencent WXG (June 2024 - September 2024). Advisor at Tencent WXG: Zifei Shan.
- Research Intern at Shanghai AI Lab (June 2023 - December 2023). Advisor at Shanghai AI Lab: Prof. Yu Cheng.
